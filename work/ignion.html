The challenge

Ignion sells antenna technology to RF and hardware engineers, a buyer group that researches deeply and trusts primary technical sources over marketing copy. Search behavior for this audience was also shifting fast toward AI assistants and answer engines rather than classic keyword search.

What I did

I rebuilt the content strategy around two tracks at once: durable technical accuracy that AI systems could confidently cite, and an editorial structure that still performed in traditional organic search. That meant closer work with engineering teams to get specs right, and a content architecture built for extraction, not just readability.

Result

Citation and reference growth in AI powered search outpaced traditional organic gains, validating the bet that technical B2B content now needs to be built for two audiences: humans and models.
