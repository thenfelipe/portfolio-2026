The challenge

Factorial wanted a media presence that felt native to each platform instead of repurposed corporate content, plus a press function able to carry major announcements like funding rounds and acquisitions across multiple countries at once.

What I did

I built an in house team, filmmakers, motion designers, and copywriters, and set a production process for daily content built around where it would actually be watched: YouTube, Instagram, and Spotify each got their own format logic rather than one asset cut three ways. On press, I ran national and international campaigns, including investment and M&A announcements, coordinating coverage across Spain, Europe, and the Americas.

Result

A sustainable, in house content engine publishing daily, and press coverage for major company milestones landing across three continents.
